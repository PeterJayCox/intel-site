AIPIO

coursecollectionframeworks
Open Source

The Australian Institute of Professional Intelligence Officers — the professional body for the intelligence profession in Australia. The wiki's course material derives from the AIPIO 25 conference (theme: Thriving Intelligence Profession), a record year with 400+ delegates.

Key themes from AIPIO 25

  • Shift to trust-based leadership and investing in ethical decision-making, critical thinking and "public-first thinking".
  • Tradecraft is more than analysis — it spans the full intelligence cycle and unique skills: cutting through uncertainty and producing action-oriented products (e.g. an intel report in two sentences, not a research paper).
  • Empowerment equation (Katheryn McMullan, AGO): leadership, strong national identity, mission focus, technology.
  • Ethical leadership (Roseleen Woodman): legality ≠ legitimacy; ethics must be structurally embedded, culture is modelled not mandated.
  • Financial intelligence (Brendan Thomas, AUSTRAC): deter, detect, disrupt; the FINTEL Alliance; typology development.
  • Partnering with AI (Kathy Pherson): complementary not replacement; right answer ≠ right reason; "confidently biased patterns".

Organisations in orbit (named at AIPIO 25)

ASD (ASD Project Redspice), AUSTRAC, AGO (geospatial), DCS, ISAC Australia, QPOL. Most of these sit inside Australia's National Intelligence Community — ASD, AUSTRAC and AGO are NIC agencies, while DCS, ISAC and QPOL sit outside it.
