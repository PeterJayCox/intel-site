analysisbias
Open Source

Judging the probability or frequency of an event by how easily instances come to mind. Vivid, recent, or dramatic events are recalled readily and are therefore over-weighted relative to their true base rate.

In intelligence analysis

  • A recent high-profile attack inflates the perceived likelihood of that attack vector, while quiescent but more probable threats are under-weighted.
  • Salient reporting (a single dramatic case) swamps aggregate statistical reporting in perceived importance.
  • Closely related to Vividness Bias — ease of recall is driven in part by vividness.

Countermeasure

  • Base-rate discipline: explicitly estimate frequencies and compare against historical baselines.
  • Structured weighting of evidence (Admiralty Rating) to separate a source's vividness from its reliability.
  • Maintain a track record and calibrate over time.
