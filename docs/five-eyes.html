Five Eyes

collectionalliancejurisdiction
Open Source

The Five Eyes (FVEY) — Australia, Canada, New Zealand, the UK and the US — is the intelligence-sharing alliance at the heart of Australia's National Intelligence Community. Australia's contribution to the UKUSA arrangements is substantial relative to its size, historically anchored in geography.

Australian facilities

  • Joint Defence Facility Pine Gap (near Alice Springs) — joint US–Australia satellite intelligence ground station
  • Kojarena (WA) — signals intelligence facility
  • Shoal Bay (NT) — signals intelligence facility
  • Facilities elsewhere

The dependence trade-off

Access to enormous partner product in exchange for hosting facilities and accepting some dependence has been a recurring topic in Australian strategic debate since Des Ball's work in the 1980s. The relationship shapes the NIC's coverage choices: the community's small size forces heavy reliance on these partnerships (National Intelligence Community).
