ASD Project Redspice

cyber-intelcollectionjurisdiction
Open Source

REDSPICE (Resilience, Effects, Defence, Space, Intelligence, Cyber, Enablers) is ASD's transformation programme, announced March 2022: roughly $9.9 billion over a decade, aimed at about doubling ASD's workforce and lifting offensive cyber, cyber security, intelligence collection and space. The Albanese government kept it. It is a flagship of the National Intelligence Community.

Why it matters analytically

ASD is no longer only the signals collector that feeds ONI and DIO. As a statutory agency (2018) it combines:

  • foreign SIGINT
  • offensive cyber operations
  • the Australian Cyber Security Centre (domestic cyber security, industry partnership, critical infrastructure)
  • a growing space role, alongside AGO

That mix is powerful and awkward: the same organisation that wants trusted relationships with banks, hospitals and telcos also conducts covert effects. Chinese and other services already treat ACSC partnership as a collection question; industry lawyers treat it as a liability question. REDSPICE makes the dual identity larger, not simpler.

Workforce

The binding constraint is people, not money: cleared cyber operators, linguists, data scientists and engineers in a market that pays better outside. ASD has to grow while competing with the firms it also needs as partners. Facilities, training pipelines and retention of mid-career staff are the unglamorous centre of the programme.

Offensive cyber accountability

Australia has been unusually public, since 2016, that it uses offensive cyber (first advertised against Islamic State). REDSPICE scales that mission. Authorisation sits in the Intelligence Services Act ministerial-direction regime, with IGIS oversight — not in a public targeting framework of the sort some Five Eyes partners have debated (Intelligence Oversight). As effects become more relevant to crisis signalling (for example in a Taiwan contingency), the gap between operational reality and public/parliamentary understanding widens — an oversight problem as much as a military one.

Is it enough?

The 2023 Defence Strategic Review and subsequent National Defence Strategy framed a much more dangerous region. REDSPICE was designed before that review's full implications, and before AUKUS Pillar II matured (AUKUS Intelligence Sharing). The live argument is whether a doubled ASD is a step-change or a catch-up, and how its growth is sequenced against Navy, cyber command-style functions in Defence, and industry.
