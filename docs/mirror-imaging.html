Mirror Imaging

analysisbias
Open Source

(Heuer) Assuming that the other actor shares the analyst's own rationality, values, and constraints — projecting one's own frame of reference onto the subject. A classic source of strategic surprise.

In intelligence analysis

  • Assuming an adversary will do what "we" would consider rational, under-estimating cultural, doctrinal, or ideological drivers.
  • Discounting that the subject may have different information, incentives, or tolerance for risk.
  • Particularly dangerous when analysing actors whose decision culture differs sharply from one's own.

Countermeasure

  • Red-team the subject's perspective on its own terms — model the decision from inside their culture and incentives.
  • Key Assumptions Check to surface the hidden assumption that "others think like us".
  • Subject-matter study of the actor's doctrine, history, and internal logic.
