Vividness Bias

analysisbias
Open Source

(Heuer's "vividness criterion"; Nisbett & Ross 1980) The tendency to over-weight concrete, emotionally striking, or graphic information over abstract but more diagnostic statistical (base-rate) information.

In intelligence analysis

  • A single dramatic case report out-weighs aggregate statistical reporting in perceived significance.
  • Visceral imagery or a compelling narrative biases threat assessment away from the base rate.
  • A primary driver of the Availability Heuristic — vivid material is more easily recalled.

Countermeasure

  • Base-rate discipline: anchor on aggregate/frequency data, then adjust for case specifics.
  • Structured weighting of sources so a source's colour does not substitute for its reliability (Admiralty Rating).
  • Explicitly separate narrative signal from statistical signal when drafting judgements.
