Anchoring Bias

Over-reliance on the first piece of information offered (the anchor) when forming subsequent estimates, with insufficient adjustment away from it — even when the anchor is arbitrary or irrelevant.
ConceptHigh confidenceanalysisbias

Availability Heuristic

Judging the probability or frequency of an event by how easily instances come to mind. Vivid, recent, or dramatic events are recalled readily and are therefore over-weighted relative to their true base rate.
ConceptHigh confidenceanalysisbias

Bottom Line Up Front

The BLUF principle — lead any intelligence product with its single most important judgment or implication, before background or detail. This is the structural core of analytic writing in the style manual.
ConceptHigh confidencewritingdissemination

Confirmation Bias

The tendency to search for, interpret, favour, and recall information in ways that confirm one's pre-existing beliefs, while giving disproportionately less weight to disconfirming evidence.
