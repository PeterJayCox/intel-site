The hover pattern, applied to the lesson itself

Item 08 in one screen. Three bias names lifted from the atlas — hover, focus or tap a name and the signature arrives without leaving the sentence. On the real page the card also carries a “read the page” link, so the lesson keeps its depth without breaking the read.

A leading hypothesis becomes self-reinforcing whenever confirmation bias is doing the reading: each new item raises confidence without any test against a rival. Watch for two observations described as a campaign — that is insensitivity to sample size, not a trend. And a negative finding written as a positive one is absence of evidence, which is a claim about our collection, not about the target.
